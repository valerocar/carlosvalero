Conversations with AI



Exchanges with AI assistants, kept close to how they happened. I save the ones where the conversation itself turned into something worth rereading — an idea followed further than I expected to take it, or turned out sharper, or stranger, than when it started.

Emergence from information exchange

From cells trading signals to ten thousand agents trading tokens — and the speed of light as the one limit that outlasts them all

Starting from the observation that connected organisms produce something bigger than themselves, the conversation follows the same pattern forward: agent swarms solving a Millennium Prize problem, self-driving labs closing the loop with the physical world, a collective that builds its own hardware, a Dyson swarm — and, once the parts are too far apart to think as one, the single mind splitting back into many.
